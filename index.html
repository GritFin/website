How it works

Most money apps show you the problem.
GRIT pays you to fix it.

You already know what you should be doing with your money. Knowing isn't the problem. Doing is. GRIT turns the right money moves into missions with real rewards.

01

Pick a mission

Choose a goal that actually matters to you. Your first mission: build a $500 emergency fund over about 90 days.

02

Build the habit

Link your bank securely and save at your own pace. GRIT tracks your progress automatically. We can see your progress, but we never touch your money.

03

Earn real cash

Hit your weekly checkpoints and finish the mission. We pay you real money. Not points. Not badges. Cash.

Then you start the next mission. That's the whole idea. Small wins that stack.

About

Why GRIT exists

At 18, I got my first credit card and made every mistake. Not because I was careless, but because no one taught me how money works. I learned the hard way, and it took years to undo those mistakes.

Most of us are in the same boat. We know what we should do. Save more, build credit, start investing. But knowing was never the problem. Doing is. Sports betting and crypto made gambling exciting. Nobody made building wealth exciting.

That's what GRIT is. We make the right money moves feel like winning, and we pay you for them. Real wealth takes grit. And grit deserves to be rewarded.

Quang Quach
Founder, GRIT Financial Inc.
Built on trust

On your side. That's the business model.

A finance app only works if you can trust it. So here's exactly how GRIT works, in plain language.

Free to use

GRIT doesn't cost you anything. When you complete a mission and you're ready for a financial product, like your first credit card or brokerage account, we connect you with a trusted partner and they pay us a referral fee. We only make money when you make progress.

No ads. No selling your data.

Ever. Our entire model depends on being the app that's actually on your side. Selling your data would break that, so we don't.

We never touch your money

Bank connections are read-only and handled by the same secure infrastructure the major finance apps use. GRIT can see your progress toward a mission. It cannot move your money.
